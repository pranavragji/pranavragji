### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an IT Engineer at the **Chair for Mathematical Foundations of Artificial Intelligence (Math4AI)**, Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München (LMU), working under Prof. Gitta Kutyniok. I hold an M.Sc. in Applied Computer Science.

My role sits at the intersection of **infrastructure engineering and applied AI** — I manage GPU clusters, networking, and hardware for the chair, while also designing and building **agentic AI systems** that automate real academic and administrative workflows.

- 🔭 Currently building an **Agentic AI platform** for Math4AI 
- 🖥️ Manage GPU compute infrastructure, local LLM deployments, and secure networked access for the research chair
- 🌱 Exploring agentic system design, local inference stacks, and analog/edge AI hardware
- ⚡ Background in HPC cluster management (SLURM), homelab virtualization, and full-stack development

---

### 🧠 Current Focus: Agentic AI & LLM Infrastructure

- Designing local-first AI stacks: **LiteLLM gateway, llama.cpp, Open WebUI, scoped MCP servers**
- Building multi-agent pipelines for document processing, budgeting, and travel workflows
- Vector/data infra: **Qdrant, PostgreSQL, Redis, MinIO**
- Evaluating inference hardware (GPU clusters, edge AI accelerators) for production LLM workloads
